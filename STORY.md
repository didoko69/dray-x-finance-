# Story studio — how every Dray x finance script is written (MANDATORY)

Goal: a TRUE story told like a thriller. The viewer is curious, shocked and keeps watching to learn what happened — and every fact is accurate (CHANNEL.md accuracy rules always win over drama).

## 1. Hook (first 30 seconds)
- Open on the single most dramatic real moment, with one big sourced number: "On March 9th, 2023, customers asked for 42 billion dollars back. In one day."
- Then the question: "How does a bank that big die in 48 hours?" Promise exactly what the title and thumbnail promised.
- Never open with background, dates lists, or "In this video".

## 2. Curiosity — open loops
- Plant 2–3 questions early (who knew? why did nobody stop it? where did the money go?). Answer one per chapter while opening a new one.
- End every chapter on a hook line: "But inside the company, one person had already found the hole." (only if true).
- Foreshadow real warnings: the ignored memo, the short seller, the auditor's question, the journalist.

## 3. People — make it human
- Every story has people with wants: the founder's ambition, the employee who noticed, the ordinary customer who lost savings.
- Show one ordinary-person moment per video (a depositor, an investor, a worker) — sourced, or clearly described as typical ("Imagine a small business owner…") without inventing a real person.
- Never mock real people. Allegations stay allegations; say when someone denies wrongdoing; give current legal status.

## 4. Explain simply
- Explain every mechanism with ONE everyday analogy (a bank run = everyone asking for their coat at once at a party with ten coats).
- No jargon without a one-line explanation. Numbers always on screen and compared to something the viewer knows.

## 5. Tension curve
- Rise (it looks amazing) → cracks (warnings ignored) → turning point (the moment it can't be hidden) → collapse (fast, minute by minute if sourced) → aftermath (who paid) → lessons.
- A twist or surprising fact around the middle that most viewers don't know.

## 6. Ending
- 3 lessons that apply to the VIEWER's own money, one closing line that lands, a comment question people want to answer ("Would you have pulled your money out?").

## 7. Writing style
- Third person, calm, confident, short sentences (6–16 words). Vary rhythm. No hype words ("insane", "crazy"), no "suddenly".
- No more than 3 lines in a row of pure numbers/explanation — break with a person, a moment or a question.

## 8. Self-review (required before building — write episodes/<slug>/story_check.md)
Score 1–10 with one line each: Hook · Curiosity · Human angle · Clarity · Surprise · Ending/lessons · Accuracy (every number sourced in sources.md).
Any score under 8 → rewrite and re-score. Accuracy must be 10 — otherwise remove the unsourced claim. Only build when all pass.
