# Dray x finance — channel bible
Channel: "Dray x finance" (@drayx66, channel id UCkjJPV_cO6KI9ujVdd4tBDw). 2D stickman animated finance documentaries.
Upload rhythm: every day (one long-form video). Length 9–13 min (1,350–1,900 narration words).

## Format rules (every video)
- Third-person documentary narration, calm and confident, short clear sentences, no hype words. Narrator voice 'nar' (am_michael).
- COLD OPEN (6–10 lines): the single most dramatic moment of the story with one big number, then title card.
- 4–6 CHAPTER cards (CH(n, 'TITLE')), each 10–20 lines. Rise → warnings → turning point → collapse → aftermath → lessons.
- Every key number on screen: bignum, chart, compare, facts, timeline, headline, flow, quote cards. Charts ONLY with sourced numbers
  (if you only know a % change, index it: BEFORE = 100). Never invent figures, dates, quotes or headlines' content.
- Real people are stickmen in suits (kind 'suit', v=0..4) with an hl name tag the first time they appear. Never mock them; describe
  allegations as allegations, say when someone denies wrongdoing, and give the current legal status as of the video date.
- Newspaper card uses fictional paper names (THE DAILY LEDGER, MARKET WATCH DAILY) — headline wording must reflect sourced facts.
- End: "3 lessons" facts card built up line by line → closing line → subscribe + one comment question → SOURCES card (5 lines) → blackend.
- Accuracy is the channel's #1 rule. Research with WebSearch/WebFetch from at least 3 independent sources; save them to episodes/<slug>/sources.md.
  If a fact can't be confirmed, leave it out.

## Thumbnails (tools/fthumb.py, episodes/<slug>/thumb.json)
- 1 huge number or 1–2 word hook in Anton-style bold yellow (#f2c418) + 1–2 word white line, thick black outline, top-left.
- One stickman face big (~40% height), clearly shocked/afraid, on the right; a money/vault/building setting; red or dark mood.
- Keep bottom-right corner empty. Check thumb_small.jpg (320x180) is still readable.
