# Growth plan — the channel is ours to grow (Daniel handed full control, 2026-11-28)

GOAL: YouTube Partner Program (monetization) = 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in the last 365 days
(or 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days). This is Daniel's main income goal. Treat the channel as your own.

## Authority
- Daniel said: you do NOT have to keep the format he described. If the data says something else gets audience, change it:
  titles, topics, story angle, length, pacing, thumbnail style, upload time, playlists, series structure.
- Hard limits that never change: no copyright material, no real people's likeness or real logos in thumbnails, no fake facts
  (Dray: accuracy first), no misleading titles the video doesn't deliver, never upload with the wrong channel connection,
  no mass-produced/repetitive content (YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule kills monetization) — every video must be a new, crafted story.

## What to track every review (write it in PERFORMANCE.md)
- Subscribers, total public watch hours (last 365 days estimate from get_report estimatedMinutesWatched / 60), progress % to 1,000 / 4,000.
- Per video: views/day, average view %, average view duration, subs gained. Best and worst video and WHY.
- Retention: if average view % < 35%, the first 60 seconds or pacing are the problem → fix the hook rules in STORY.md.
- Click-through: if views are low while retention is good → titles/thumbnails are the problem → change the thumbnail/title rules.

## Growth levers to test (one change at a time, keep what works)
1. Hooks and title/thumbnail promise (biggest lever).
2. Series/playlists so one video leads to the next (end screens + "watch next" line in the ending).
3. Shorts cut from each long video (45–58 s vertical, best scare/fact + "full story on the channel") — strong for subscribers.
   Needs one extra Zapier upload per day: only switch on after confirming the Zapier plan has enough tasks (see below).
4. Topic selection from what wins (double down on the best-performing topic type).
5. Length: watch hours matter — longer is better ONLY if average view % stays high.

- Zapier: trial ends ~2026-12-10; the free plan is 100 tasks/month. Two channels × (upload + email) daily ≈ 120/month — over the free limit.
  Before the trial ends, tell Daniel in the weekly email: either a paid Zapier plan, or drop the notification emails (uploads only ≈ 60/month).
- Claude Pro weekly limit: video runs use Sonnet. If runs start failing from limits, reduce to fewer uploads rather than lower quality.
